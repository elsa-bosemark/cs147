The idea

For the love of
making, again.

Etch is for makers who love their craft but have lost the joy in it. Each morning, Etch gives you three short prompts to sketch outside on paper, and every sketch grows into a flower to gift. A bouquet for a friend gives your work a purpose again, and a bouquet for yourself, opened months later, reminds you why you made it.

Make something.
Let it grow.

Nº 01

Pick a seed.

Every day, Etch offers three small sketching prompts called seeds. Each seed grows into a different kind of flower.

Nº 02

Sketch it on paper.

Head outside with any pen and paper. When you finish, take a photo of your sketch and of what inspired it. Etch plants the sketch, and over the next few days it grows into a flower.

Nº 03

Gift what grows.

Gather a few of your flowers into a bouquet and add a note. Send it to a friend, or schedule it to reach you months from now.
